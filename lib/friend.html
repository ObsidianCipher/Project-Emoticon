transmission date: always // to: you

There are people who walk through your life, and people who become your life — the ones who rearrange something quietly inside you and then act like they didn't even notice.


You are the second kind.


I've been thinking about how to explain what your friendship means — and every time I try, language feels too small. Too flat. You deserve something bigger than words, but words are what I have, so: thank you.


Thank you for the real conversations. For the times you chose honesty over comfort. For seeing my worst days and staying anyway.


For laughing with me — genuinely, stupidly, fully.


For being someone I don't have to perform for.


The universe is mostly empty space. And somehow, you ended up in mine. I don't take that lightly.


So — thank you. For being exactly who you are. For being my friend.

— with everything I've got.
P.S. · this is not nearly enough. but it's the truest thing I know how to say.
